# Design notes

Write this as you build, not at the end. It is also your system design practice.

## Problem
What this system has to do, and the constraints that make it non-trivial.

## Architecture
Diagram plus a walk through the request path.

## Options considered and rejected
For each major choice: what you picked, what you rejected, and the reason.

## Failure modes
What breaks, how it is detected, what happens to the user.

## What changes at 10x
The first bottleneck, and what you would do about it.
